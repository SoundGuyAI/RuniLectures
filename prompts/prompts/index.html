Where it started

One message, one brief

On the evening of November 12, 2026, the instructor sent one message that defined the whole project: the course, the four countries, the deliverables, the style, and the way it should be built. From that message, verbatim:

“make all the presentation interactive sites. with a index page leading to all of them.”
“i want the presetations to be fun and juicy and flashing and interactie and not just words or slides. add also voice overs in english. too all presetnations. also make a motoin video for each one of the presentations.”
“i want you to document all my prompts and the workflow so the students will know how I use AI agents”

That last request is why this page exists. Out of the brief came five decks (Colombia, Czech Republic, Nigeria, Sri Lanka, and the AI workflow session), a narrated motion video for each, an index page, and a repo students can use as a template.

What to notice

How to work like this

  1. Describe the outcome, not the implementation. “Fun and juicy and flashing” is a target, not a spec; the agents chose the techniques.
  2. Review the real thing. Every correction above came from looking at an actual page on his phone, not from planning documents.
  3. Correct in plain language. “Too generic”, “looks like a continuous page”, “more visuals”: short reactions steer better than long briefs.
  4. Delegate once the direction is right. When the decks matched his taste, he skipped the text review and let the agents run.
  5. Document the process itself. This page exists because one of the prompts asked for it.
